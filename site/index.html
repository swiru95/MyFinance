The problem

Your money lives in a dozen places, so three simple questions go unanswered.

What am I worth today?

Cash in the bank, ETFs at a broker, coins on an exchange and gold in a safe, all in different units and currencies.

Where did the money go?

Budgets only catch what you remember to type in. The rest disappears quietly.

How much risk am I taking?

A market drawdown and an illiquid watch collection are different risks, and a spreadsheet treats them the same.

Two axes, not one

Volatility and liquidity are measured separately. A watch collection won't crash, but it can't pay next month's rent either, so it gets its own band instead of inflating "risky".

Your safety net comes from it

Reserve cover counts only the safe band against your monthly commitments. Split the value chart by risk to see whether that band stays above the six-month line.

Each asset has its own band

Every asset starts in its class's band but keeps its own, so one holding can be classed differently from the rest. The AI review is told the bands too.

Effective spend

effective spend =
income − change in net worth

Everything you earn either ends up in your portfolio or gets spent. MyFinance works out your real spending from your net worth, including everything you forgot to log. It's honest about markets too: a drawdown counts in the month it happens, and the app tells you so.

Wallet assessment

A private portfolio review, on your own hardware

Tell it what kind of investor you want to be. A language model then checks what you actually hold against that goal and says plainly where the two differ.

  • Qwen3.5 122B writes the review; Bielik 11B translates it into natural Polish
  • The request goes to your own GPU server, not a cloud API
  • Runs in the background, since a large model takes minutes
  • Each report is stored with the figures it was based on, so an old review still makes sense months later

Four styles, four yardsticks

Safe

Capital preservation first. Expects at least six months of cover and is strict about concentration in stocks, crypto or any single holding.

Balanced

Real equity exposure plus a three-to-six-month buffer. No single class should dominate, and speculative holdings stay a modest slice.

Risky

Drawdowns and conviction bets are accepted, but it still insists on enough cash that a bad year never forces a sale at the bottom.

Long-term

A horizon of ten years or more. Idle cash and fees count as real costs, and each holding has to make sense a decade from now.

What the model sees

  • Every holding with its value and share, rolled up by class and by risk band
  • Monthly commitments by category
  • Twelve months of income, recorded spend and effective spend, plus your savings rate
  • The same numbers the dashboard shows

What you get back, and the rules it follows

  • Summary, What stands out, Risks and Recommendations, in that order
  • Every recommendation names a target: a percentage, an amount or months of cover
  • It uses only your figures, never invents a number, and says when something is missing
  • It never recommends specific tickers, funds or products. It's analysis, not financial advice.

Security

Security built in from the start

Entra ID sign-on

Authorization code with PKCE. There's no client secret in the code, the chart or the image.

Role-gated access

Only accounts assigned the app role get in, not everyone in the tenant.

Mutual TLS

The backend proves its identity with a certificate to PostgreSQL and to the model server.

Self-hosted

Your financial data stays on your own server or cluster. There's no third-party SaaS.
